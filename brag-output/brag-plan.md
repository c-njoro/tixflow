# Tixflow — organiser launch video

**What it is:** Ticketing for Kenyan events: sell with M-Pesa, cards and instalments, run the gate with scanning that catches copied tickets even offline, and get paid out to M-Pesa or bank.
**Who for:** Event organisers in Kenya who currently run on "a group chat and a cash box".
**What sets it apart:** Lipa Pole Pole instalments, re-entry scanning that flags copied tickets, offline gate mode, payouts on request, no monthly fee (5% per ticket).
**Hook:** The site's own three-beat promise, landing word by word: *Sell tickets. Run the gate. Get paid.*
**Punchline:** "Your next event deserves better than a group chat and a cash box." (real copy from /organisers)
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt

**Tone:** `default` leaning polished: confident, clean, product-led. Soft transitions, dips through the ink background.
**Identity:** ink #0B0F17, panel #0E131F, deep #080A10, slate text, emerald-600 / amber-500 / rose-600 / sky-600 for state. Bricolage Grotesque (display), Geist (UI), Geist Mono (codes). Real Tailwind classes from the app, compiled with the project's Tailwind v4.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, ~21.5s.

## Sound
112 BPM, A minor. Soft four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat shaker, warm Am–F–C–G pad, sub bass. SFX in key: typing ticks (quiet), a two-note rising A–E chime for "Admitted", a soft falling E–C for "Already inside", a gentle whoosh on scene changes. Everything sits under the music.
