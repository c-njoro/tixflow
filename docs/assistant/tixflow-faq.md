# About Tixflow

What is Tixflow? Tixflow is a ticketing platform for events in Kenya. People use it to find events and buy tickets with M-Pesa or a card. Organisers use it to sell tickets, check people in at the gate with QR scanning, run live polls, Q&A and slides during the event, and get paid out to M-Pesa or a bank account.

Who is Tixflow for? Two groups. Ticket buyers, who browse events and buy tickets on the Tixflow website. Event organisers (concerts, conferences, festivals, church and school events, workshops), who create an account, publish their events and manage everything from the Tixflow dashboard.

Does Tixflow organise the events? No. Each event is run by its own organiser, whose name is shown on the event page. Tixflow provides the tickets, payments, check-in and event tools. Questions about the event itself (line-up, dress code, parking, age limits, programme changes) are for the organiser.

# Finding and buying tickets

How do I find events? The Tixflow homepage lists upcoming events, soonest first, and you can filter by category or search by name, category or location. Each event has its own page with the date, venue, ticket types and prices.

How do I buy a ticket? Open the event page, choose your ticket types and quantities, then fill in your first name, last name and email. You can also add a WhatsApp number (optional). Pay with M-Pesa or a card. Your tickets are sent as soon as the payment goes through.

Which payment methods are accepted? M-Pesa, through an STK push prompt sent to your phone, and cards (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay). Some events also offer Lipa Pole Pole, which lets you pay in instalments.

How does M-Pesa payment work? Enter the M-Pesa number you will pay from. A prompt pops up on that phone asking for your M-Pesa PIN. Enter it, and your tickets are issued once Safaricom confirms the payment. Keep the page open until it confirms.

I didn't get the M-Pesa prompt. What do I do? Check that the M-Pesa number you entered is correct and that your phone is on and unlocked, then try again. An order whose prompt was never sent expires after about 10 minutes, and you will not be charged for it.

I paid but I haven't received my ticket. What should I do? Wait a few minutes first. Tixflow checks with Safaricom on any payment that is still pending and issues the tickets automatically. Then check your email, including the spam and promotions folders. You can also use "Find my tickets" on the website, or ask this assistant to send you a link to your tickets.

Are there booking fees? On paid events there may be a small booking fee per ticket. The organiser decides whether buyers pay it or the organiser covers it. If it applies, it is shown as "Booking fee" in the order summary before you pay. Free tickets have no fee.

Can I use a promo code? Yes, if the organiser has created one. Enter it in the "Promo code" box at checkout and press Apply. The discount shows in the order summary before you pay. Codes can be a fixed amount or a percentage off, and some have limits set by the organiser.

How do I get a free ticket? On a free event, choose your tickets and press "Get my free ticket". No payment is needed. The ticket is sent to your email (and WhatsApp, if you gave a number) straight away.

# Lipa Pole Pole (pay in instalments)

What is Lipa Pole Pole? Lipa Pole Pole lets you pay for tickets in instalments, on events where the organiser has switched it on. You pay a deposit at checkout to reserve your seats, then pay the rest in any amounts before a deadline set by the organiser.

How do I use Lipa Pole Pole? At checkout, choose M-Pesa and tick "Lipa Pole Pole — pay in instalments". Enter a deposit of at least the minimum shown (the organiser sets the minimum percentage). You get a link to your payment plan page, where you can see what you have paid and top up any time by M-Pesa.

What is the smallest top-up? KES 50, or whatever is left to pay if that is less.

When do I get my tickets on Lipa Pole Pole? As soon as the plan is fully paid. Your seats are reserved from the moment the deposit goes through.

What happens if I don't finish paying in time? You get reminders 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before the deadline. If the plan is not fully paid by the deadline, it expires and the reserved seats are released back on sale.

Can I start Lipa Pole Pole close to the event? Only while the organiser's deadline is more than a day away. After that, the option no longer shows at checkout.

# Getting and using your ticket

How are tickets delivered? By email, with your designed ticket card attached (it includes the QR code). If you gave a WhatsApp number at checkout, the tickets are sent there too. Tickets bought at the door can also be sent by SMS.

What do I show at the entrance? The QR code on your ticket. Show it on your phone screen or printed. Gate staff scan it with a phone or a handheld scanner.

Can I download my ticket? Yes. Each ticket has a "Download ticket" button on its ticket page, and the ticket card is attached to your confirmation email.

I lost my ticket email. How do I get my tickets back? Use "Find my tickets" (the "My tickets" link at the top of the website). Enter the email you used when buying, and optionally the WhatsApp number you gave at checkout. A link to view all your tickets is sent to that email, and to WhatsApp if the number matches your checkout. You can also ask this assistant to send the link. For your security, the link only ever goes to the email and WhatsApp number used when buying, never to a different address, and it expires after 15 minutes. If it has expired, just request a new one.

I didn't get the "Find my tickets" link. Why? Check the spam and promotions folders, and check the email is spelled exactly as you typed it at checkout. If you might have used another email, try that one. For privacy, Tixflow never says whether an email has tickets, so the message looks the same either way. Requests are limited to a few per email every 15 minutes, so if you just asked several times, wait a little before trying again.

Can my ticket be used twice? Each ticket gets in once. If the organiser allows re-entry, you can be scanned out and back in up to the number of times they set. A copied ticket that is already inside is flagged at the gate as "Already inside — possible copied ticket", so don't share your QR code.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else? Tixflow does not currently have a ticket transfer feature. Ask the event's organiser about their policy.

Will I get a reminder before the event? Yes. Ticket holders get a reminder the day before and about 2 hours before the start, by email and WhatsApp, with a map link to the venue.

# Refunds

Can I get a refund? You can request one, and the event organiser decides. Open your tickets with "Find my tickets", choose the tickets, give a reason and the M-Pesa number to refund to. The organiser approves or declines. If they approve, the tickets are cancelled and the money is sent to your M-Pesa.

What is refunded? What you paid for the tickets themselves, after any promo discount. The booking fee is not refunded.

When can I request a refund? Any time before the event starts. Once it has started, contact the organiser directly.

Which tickets can't be refunded online? Free tickets, tickets paid in cash at the door, and complimentary tickets, because they weren't paid for online. Contact the organiser about those. Request refunds for one event at a time.

How do I know where my refund is? Your tickets page shows the refund status: "Refund requested — waiting for the organiser", "Refund approved — being sent to your M-Pesa", "Refund on its way to your M-Pesa", "Refunded", or "Refund declined".

# During the event: Event Space

What is Event Space? Event Space is a live page for the people inside an event or conference room. You open it on your phone and can answer live polls, follow the presenter's slides page by page, read announcements, and ask and upvote questions for the speakers.

How do I join an Event Space? Scan the QR code shown on the screen in the room, or open the link sent to ticket holders by email and WhatsApp. The links usually arrive about an hour before the event starts. If you buy a ticket after that, they come with your ticket. You don't need an account.

How do live polls work? When the organiser starts a poll, it appears on the Live tab. Pick an answer (or type one) and press Submit. Results can show live on the big screen and on your phone.

What is "Following live"? When the organiser presents a document, your phone follows along and moves to whatever page they are on. You can flip pages yourself, and press "Back to live" to rejoin the presenter.

How does Q&A work? On the Q&A tab, type a question for the speakers (your name is optional) and press Ask. Upvote other people's questions so the most popular ones rise to the top. Some organisers approve questions before they appear. The organiser can put a question on the big screen.

Can I switch rooms? Yes. Multi-room events and conferences show "Other rooms" at the top of the Event Space so you can move between parallel tracks.

# After the event

Will I get a certificate of attendance? If the organiser offers certificates, attendees whose ticket was scanned in at the gate get a personal certificate link after the event. You can set the name printed on it.

What is the feedback survey? Organisers can send a short survey after the event. Each attendee gets a personal link, and responses are anonymous to the organiser.

# For organisers: getting started

How do I start selling tickets on Tixflow? Click "Create event" and register your business. It takes a couple of minutes, with no paperwork and no approval wait. Then build your event: details, images, ticket types, prices, door prices and re-entry rules. Publish it and share the link on socials, WhatsApp, posters and through promoters.

Can I run more than one event? Yes. There is no limit on the number of events or ticket types under your account.

Can I sell different ticket types? Yes: Early Bird, Regular, VIP, tables and more. Each ticket type has its own price, capacity and door price.

# For organisers: pricing

How much does Tixflow cost? There is no setup fee and no monthly fee. On paid events Tixflow takes 5% per ticket sold, with a minimum of KES 20 a ticket. You choose whether buyers pay it on top as a booking fee or it comes out of your sales. Box-office cash sales are charged too, deducted from your payout.

What does a paid event include? Everything: unlimited events, ticket types and promo codes, 3 Event Space rooms (polls, Q&A, slides), certificates, exhibitor lead scanning, gate scanning with re-entry, the box office, offline mode, and M-Pesa, card and Lipa Pole Pole payments.

How much do free events cost? Free events are free up to the Free plan's limits. For more, buy a plan for that one event by M-Pesa or card.
- Free: up to 300 people, 1 Event Space room for up to 100 people at once. KES 0.
- Plus: up to 1,000 people, 3 rooms for up to 500 people each, plus certificates and exhibitor lead scanning. KES 2,500 per event.
- Pro: unlimited people, 10 rooms with no room size limit, plus certificates and exhibitor lead scanning. KES 6,000 per event.
Free tickets and complimentary tickets on a paid event count towards these limits, and the first 300 are included.

# For organisers: getting paid

How do I get paid? Buyers' M-Pesa and card payments come to Tixflow, and you withdraw your balance whenever you are ready. Add your M-Pesa number or bank details once, then press "Request payout" in your dashboard. You confirm with a code sent to your email, the payout is approved, and the money is sent to your M-Pesa or bank. You don't need to set up a separate payment account.

Can I see my sales live? Yes. The dashboard shows tickets sold, revenue and arrivals as they happen.

How do refunds work for organisers? Buyers request refunds from their ticket page. You approve or decline. Approved tickets are cancelled, the seats go back on sale, and Tixflow sends the refund to the buyer's M-Pesa from your balance. The booking fee is not refunded.

# For organisers: promoters and promo codes

How do promo codes work? Create codes such as MUKURU for a set amount or a percentage off. A code can be tied to a promoter, so their sales are tracked.

How do promoters work? Each promoter gets their own link. Sales through that link (or their promo code) earn them the commission you set. Commission is held back from your withdrawable balance and paid to the promoter's M-Pesa through Tixflow.

# For organisers: at the gate

How does check-in work? Gate staff scan tickets with a phone camera or a handheld scanner, and the screen shows instantly whether a ticket is valid, already used, or not recognised. A live gate dashboard shows arrivals, who is inside and scans by each staff member.

Can I control re-entry? Yes. You set how many re-entries each ticket gets (or none). Staff scan people out and back in, and a copied ticket that is already inside is caught.

What if the internet goes down at the venue? Download the ticket list on the gate screen before the gates open. Scanning then keeps working without signal and syncs when the connection is back.

Can I sell tickets at the door? Yes, with the box office. Sell walk-in tickets at your door price for cash or M-Pesa, then print the ticket (58mm receipt printers) or send it by SMS.

Can my staff scan without seeing my payouts? Yes. Add staff accounts that can only scan tickets, separate from admin access to your events and payouts.

Can I rent scanners and gate staff? Yes. Tixflow rents handheld scanners with a built-in barcode reader and receipt printer at KES 1,500 per day, and trained scanning staff at KES 2,000 per person per day. Request them from your event's Plan & Gear page.

# For organisers: conferences and engagement

What tools are there for conferences? Event Space rooms with live polls, Q&A with upvotes, document presenting where attendees' phones follow your page, announcements and a projector screen you control from the dashboard. There are also certificates of attendance, post-event feedback surveys, and exhibitor lead scanning, where exhibitors scan attendees' ticket QR codes at their stand with a phone to collect leads.

How do I run an Event Space room? Create a room for your event, open the projector screen on the laptop connected to the venue screen, and choose what it shows from the dashboard: the join QR code, a live poll, Q&A or a document. Ticket holders get the room link automatically before the event.
