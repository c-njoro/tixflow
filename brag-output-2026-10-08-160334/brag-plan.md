# Tixflow Event Space — inside the room

**What it is:** Every Tixflow event comes with Event Space, a live companion for the room: attendees scan a QR on the projector, then answer live polls, follow the presenter's slides page by page, and ask and upvote questions on their phones.
**Who for:** Conference and event organisers, and the speakers or hosts running the room.
**What sets it apart:** The organiser drives the projector *and* every phone from one control room. Polls fill the big screen as people tap. Press Next and every phone that's following moves to the same page. Rooms for parallel tracks, with 3 included on paid events.
**Hook:** The projector join screen. A QR fills the frame, then pulls back while the "people here" count runs up: *The whole room, live on every phone.*
**Angle:** One room, two screens in sync: the projector on the left and an attendee's phone on the right. Whatever happens on one shows up on the other.
**Punchline:** "Polls, Q&A and slides, in every room." Plus *3 Event Space rooms with every paid event* (real copy from the pricing page).

**Tone:** `default`, clean and lively. Soft transitions that dip through ink.
**Identity:** same as the app. The projector is pure black (the real screen.tsx), the phone uses ink #0B0F17 and panels #0E131F. Sky-400 for the leading poll bar and spotlight, emerald for "Following live". Bricolage Grotesque, Geist, Geist Mono.

**Demo content (illustrative):** the event "AgriTech Summit 2026" by Kilimo Labs, the room "Main Hall" with other rooms "Workshop Room" and "Hall B", the deck "Keynote: Data on the farm".

## Sound
118 BPM in D minor, a little brighter than the first video: Dm–B♭–F–C pad, soft kick from bar 2, pluck arpeggio. Effects in key: a soft pop for each join early on (kept in the background), a tap and a rising D–A chime on Submit, a paper-soft swish on the page turn, a gentle F–A tick on the upvote, and whooshes into each scene.
